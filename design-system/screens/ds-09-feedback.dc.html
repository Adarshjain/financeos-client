Callout
Feedback and overlays

Inline messages inside a page or sheet. `note` is neutral information on `bg-canvas`; `callout` is something to act on, on `warning-tint` with `warning-ink` text.

Copy

Say what happened and what to do, with the specific numbers: "2 bank credits look like dividends but are not recorded … Reconcile". No apologies, no "Note:" prefix.

Don't

Don't use a callout for something the user did not ask about (no unprompted warnings about statement cut-offs).

Variants

  • Callout (needs action), note (neutral), and the warning banner used inside cards.

Banner
Feedback and overlays

An inline message at the top of a form or list: a failed request, a success that needs no action, or information about how the screen was reached.

Tones

  • err on danger-tint with danger-ink: "Email or password is incorrect.", "Failed to load upcoming items. Try again".
  • ok on brand-tint: "Your account has been deleted.", "Account created! Redirecting…".
  • info on info-tint: "That item is already handled."
  • warn on warning-tint: lines skipped in a statement, a merge that should be a link.

Rule

Say what happened and what to do next. Keep the form and its values; never clear what the user typed. No icon: the tone colour and the words are enough.

Toast
Feedback and overlays

A short confirmation at the bottom of the screen after an action, with Undo when the action can be reversed.

Use

  • Says what happened, with the object: "Snoozed until Wed 14 Oct", "Marked ₹20,000 paid · ₹13,900 still due", "Dashboard deleted".
  • Offer Undo for snooze, dismiss, mark paid and other reversible actions; the row leaves at once and Undo puts it back.
  • Failures use the error mark and say what is back the way it was: "Couldn't snooze. The row is back."
  • One toast at a time, about 5 seconds, above the tab bar on phone.
  • No icon: the words carry the result. A failure says so in its first words ("Couldn't …").

Don't

  • Don't use a toast for something the user must act on; use a Banner or the Inbox.

EmptyState
Feedback and overlays

What a screen shows when it has nothing yet, or cannot load: a small illustration, a `screen-title` headline, one sentence, and the one action that fixes it.

Use

  • Empty list: say what will appear and offer the first step ("No transactions yet" · Add account).
  • Offline: a friendly generic page (sleeping ₹ coin under a cloud), no cached data, one "Try again" button.
  • Errors: what failed and the reference to quote; never a stack trace.

Variants

  • First run, nothing matches (filters), all clear, couldn't load, offline, and the one-line empty used inside small panels.

Skeleton
Feedback and overlays

Loading placeholders shaped like the content they stand for: grey bars in `line-soft` at the heights of the text they replace.

Use

Show for loads over about 300ms. Keep the real layout (card header, rows) so nothing jumps when data arrives. Respect reduced motion: no shimmer.

Variants

  • Rows, a single figure, widget rows, table rows.

Sheet
Feedback and overlays

On phone every dialog is a bottom sheet: rounded top corners (`radius-sheet`), a drag handle, title and subtitle, scrolling body, sticky footer.

Rules

  • No × button on phone: swipe down or tap the scrim to close. Desktop dialogs keep ×.
  • The body is the only scroller; the footer stays pinned with full-width buttons (btn-xl), primary on the right.
  • Detail sheets list facts as fld rows and put actions in one row near the top (Edit · Link to… · ✓ Review · Delete).
  • Open dropdowns inside a sheet open as a second sheet over it.

Variants

  • Form, detail (actions row + label/value rows), picker (a long Select on phone), result summary (counts, then the rows that were skipped or failed). Confirmations use ConfirmSheet.

ConfirmSheet
Feedback and overlays

The confirm step before anything destructive or hard to undo: delete a transaction, statement, account, dashboard, category or rule; discard unsaved edits; delete older Gmail rows.

Anatomy

Title names the object ("Delete Swiggy Bangalore · ₹1,249?"), one or two sentences say exactly what goes and what stays, then Cancel and the action. The action uses btn-dd (filled danger) and repeats the verb: "Delete statement", "Delete & save".

Use

  • When the server can tell what will be affected, show its preview first ("6 transactions it created will be deleted…") with a loading line while it checks.
  • Offer the gentler option when there is one: "Close instead" on Delete account.
  • Discard confirms read "Keep editing" / "Discard"; losing edits counts as destructive, so Discard is btn-dd too.

Dialog
Feedback and overlays

The desktop form of a sheet: centred, `radius-xl`, `shadow-dialog`, × at the top right, footer right-aligned on `bg-sunken`.

Use

  • Widths: 440 small (confirm), 640 default, 760 large (pickers), 920 extra large (tables).
  • Destructive confirms name the object and the consequence: "Delete HDFC Regalia Gold? 142 transactions will be deleted with it."
  • A destructive secondary action may sit at the far left of the footer (Delete rule).

Variants

  • Small (440) for confirms and short forms, default (640), large (760) for pickers and lists, extra large (920) for tables.

AuthPanel
Feedback and overlays

Login and sign-up: an illustrated mint panel (on phone a short strip on top) and a narrow form column, max 380px.

Anatomy

  • Panel: illus-mint ground with floating tiles (radius-xl, shadow-raise), a brand card and a gold coin. Shapes only; no stock photos.
  • Form: screen-title headline, one line of copy, "Continue with Google" (gbtn, 44px), an "or" divider, email and password, primary button full width.
  • Sign-up adds an invite code field and a three-step explainer.
