Banner
Feedback

The one inline message. It replaces the old Callout and Note: a failed request at the top of a form, a success that needs no action, how the screen was reached, something to act on inside a card, or a neutral note. Toast is the same message floating.

Tones

  • danger on danger-tint with danger-ink: "Email or password is incorrect."
  • success on positive-tint with positive-strong: "Your account has been deleted."
  • warning on warning-tint with warning-ink: something to act on. "2 bank credits look like dividends. Reconcile"
  • info on info-tint with info-ink: "That item is already handled."
  • neutral on bg-surface with a line border and ink-2 text: a note ("Changing the pattern unlinks the 31 transactions it categorised.").

Props

tone, children (the message), optional title (bold lead-in), optional action (a link-styled button at the end: "Reconcile", "See them", "Try again").

Layout

  • One line of text keeps the banner one line tall: text and action are vertically centred, and the action is Button link (inline text, no padding), so adding an action never changes the height.
  • Longer text wraps; the action stays at the end of the last line.

Rules

  • Say what happened and what to do next. Keep the form and its values.
  • No icon: the tone and the words carry it (RULES §6).
  • Placement is layout, not a variant: top of a form, top of a list, or inside a card.

Built from

Button link (the action). Toast reuses its tones.

Toast
Feedback

A Banner that floats at the bottom of the screen after an action, for about 5 seconds, with Undo when the action can be reversed.

Tones

The same as Banner, so a toast reads the same as an inline message: neutral (the default: "Snoozed until Wed 14 Oct"), success, danger ("Couldn't snooze. The row is back."), warning, info. Floating adds shadow-pop.

Use

  • Says what happened, with the object: "Marked ₹20,000 paid · ₹13,900 still due", "Dashboard deleted".
  • Undo for snooze, dismiss, mark paid and other reversible actions; the row leaves at once and Undo puts it back.
  • One toast at a time, above the tab bar on phone, bottom centre on desktop.
  • No icon.

Toaster

  • Toaster is the one mount point for new-system toasts: bottom centre, above the TabBar on phone (bottom-safe-12 plus the bar), a single toast at a time. It is a separate Sonner toaster (id="ds"); showToast always targets it, so new toasts never appear in the old app's top-centre toaster and old toasts never appear in the new one. New layouts mount <Toaster /> once.

Built from

Banner (tones and layout), Sonner (queue and timing), Button link (Undo).

Don't

  • Don't use a toast for something the user must act on; use a Banner or the Inbox.

EmptyState
Feedback

What a screen shows when it has nothing yet, or cannot load: a small illustration, a `screen-title` headline, one sentence, and the one action that fixes it.

Use

  • Empty list: say what will appear and offer the first step ("No transactions yet" · Add account).
  • Offline: a friendly generic page (sleeping ₹ coin under a cloud), no cached data, one "Try again" button.
  • Errors: what failed and the reference to quote; never a stack trace.

Built from

Button, Illustration (offline).

Skeleton
Feedback

Loading placeholders shaped like the content they stand for: grey bars in `line-soft` at the heights of the text they replace.

Use

Show for loads over about 300ms. Keep the real layout (card header, rows) so nothing jumps when data arrives. Respect reduced motion: no shimmer.

Built from

Nothing else.

Variants

  • Rows, a single figure, widget rows, table rows.

Illustration
Feedback

The flat artwork on the sign-in, sign-up, offline and empty screens, as one component so pages never draw art themselves. Shapes from the palette only: `illus-mint` ground, tint blobs (`positive-tint`, `warning-tint`, `info-tint`), white tiles (`bg-surface`, `line`, `shadow-raise`), the card in `brand`, the coin in `illus-gold` with `illus-gold-edge`. No photos, people or mascots. Decorative, hidden from screen readers.

Variants

  • signin: the desktop left panel of Sign in: logo, the scene (a card, a net-worth tile, a bill-paid tile, a points coin), the line "Every rupee, every card, every loan. One calm ledger." with its two-line copy and the trust line. On phone the copy hides and the scene shrinks to a band above the form.
  • signup: the same panel for Sign up: the scene (statement imported, Gmail connected, card advice, a lending reminder) and the three setup steps.
  • offline: the sleeping ₹ coin under a cloud (used by EmptyState's offline variant).

Phone

  • Each variant has a phone form: the scene shrinks to a band above the form and the copy hides. Storybook shows it as its own story at 390px (signin · phone, signup · phone).

Built from

Logo (in the sign-in and sign-up panels). EmptyState uses the offline variant.
